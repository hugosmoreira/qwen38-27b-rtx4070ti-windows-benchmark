# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, safe, and equitable. Everyone participating in good faith receives the same opportunity to contribute, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or other status.

## Expected behavior

Project participants are expected to:

- engage kindly, honestly, and professionally;
- respect different viewpoints and experiences;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback gracefully;
- credit sources and distinguish evidence from opinion;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm; and
- protect private information, credentials, unpublished prompts, and other sensitive material.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is unacceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention;
- insulting, demeaning, discriminatory, or sexualized comments or imagery;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- impersonation or attempts to evade moderation;
- knowingly misleading benchmark claims, fabricated evidence, or missing source attribution;
- promotional or commercial material unrelated to the project's purpose; and
- conduct that would reasonably threaten the safety or well-being of project participants.

## Reporting an issue

Report possible violations privately to Hugo Moreira at [hugo@webstudiolabs.com](mailto:hugo@webstudiolabs.com) with `Code of Conduct` in the subject line. Do not disclose a sensitive conduct report in a public GitHub issue.

Reports should include the relevant dates, project spaces, links or records, people involved, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Details will be shared only when necessary to investigate, protect participants, or carry out an enforcement decision.

Hugo Moreira is the initial project moderator. If a report concerns the moderator or presents a conflict of interest, reporters may use the applicable hosting platform's trust-and-safety or abuse-reporting process instead. The project does not claim to have an independent moderation team at this release stage.

## Addressing violations

The moderator may use the following actions according to impact, context, and history:

1. **Private correction:** clarify expectations and request corrective action.
2. **Warning:** issue a documented warning and conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** limit participation or contact for a defined period.
4. **Permanent removal:** remove access to project spaces when lesser measures cannot protect the community or when a severe violation warrants immediate action.

Enforcement decisions will prioritize participant safety, proportionality, privacy, and a clear explanation to the affected parties. A public summary may be provided only when it can be done without exposing reporters or other sensitive information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project-associated communication, and situations where someone is officially representing the project.
